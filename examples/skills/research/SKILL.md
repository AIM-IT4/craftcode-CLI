# Research brief

1. Restate the exact research question and decision it supports.
2. Inventory only the sources/data actually available.
3. Separate observed evidence from assumptions and inference.
4. Prefer targeted retrieval over broad ingestion.
5. End with findings, caveats and the next highest-value check.
